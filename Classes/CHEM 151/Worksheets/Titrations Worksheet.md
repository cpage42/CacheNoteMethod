## Weak Acid–Strong Base Titration

Consider the titration of 100.0 mL of 0.5 M hypothetical weak acid "HA" (where $\mathrm{p}K_a = 3.75$) with 1.00 M KOH.

a. What is the $K_a$ of this weak acid (no base added, just the weak acid)?
$$
10^{-3.75}=1.78\times 10^{-4}
$$

b. Calculate the initial pH of the weak acid (HA) solution (no base added yet)?

$$
\begin{align}
 & HA+H_{2}O\rightleftharpoons H_{3}O^{+}+A^{-} \\
 & I:0.5,0,0 \\
 & C:-x,+x,+x \\
 & E:0.5-x,x,x \\
 & pH=-\log[H_{3}O^{+}]=-\log x \\
 & K_{a}=\frac{x^{2}}{0.5-x}\approx 2x^{2}\to x\approx 0.00943\to pH=2.03
\end{align}
$$

c. What is the pH after the addition of 10 mL of KOH?

$$
\begin{align}
 & HA+OH^{-}\to A^{-}+H_{2}O \\
 & \text{starting moles:} \\
 & mol(HA)=0.5\times 0.1=0.05mol \\
 & mol(OH^{-})=1.0\times 0.01=0.01mol \\
 & \begin{bmatrix}
 i \\
 c \\
 e
 \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}
 0.05 & 0.01 & 0 \\
 -0.01 & -0.01 & +0.01 \\
 0.04 & 0 & 0.01
 \end{bmatrix}\to x=0.01,\text{ b/c OH reacts completely.} \\
  & \text{henderson-hasselbach:} \\
  & pH=pK_{a}+\log \frac{A^{-}}{HA}
\end{align}
$$
