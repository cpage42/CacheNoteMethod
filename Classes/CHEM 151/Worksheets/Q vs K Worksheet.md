## Reaction 1: n-butane ⇌ isobutane

```smiles
CCCC>>CC(C)C
```
*n-butane (left) ⇌ isobutane / 2-methylpropane (right). Both are C₄H₁₀.*

$$\text{n-butane}\ \rightleftharpoons\ \text{isobutane}$$

**1.** If the above reaction has a $K_c$ value of 2.5 at 298 K, which way will the reaction go if you start with all n-butane?

**Answer:**  reaction will go right until it hits equilibrium.

**2.** If you start the above reaction with 10 M isobutane and 2 M n-butane, what is the value of $Q_c$?

**Answer:** ${}\frac{[\text{isobutane}]^{1}}{[\text{n-butane}]^{1}}=\frac{10}{2}=5{}$

**3.** Under these conditions, which way will the reaction move to establish equilibrium?

**Answer:** ${}Q>K \therefore \text{reaction moves towards reactant side}{}$

**4.** What are the concentrations of isobutane and n-butane when equilibrium is established?

**Answer:** ${}\begin{bmatrix}R & \text{n-butane} &  \text{isobutane} \\  I & 2M & 10M \\  C & +x & -x \\  E & 2+x & 10-x\end{bmatrix} \implies \frac{10-x}{2+x}=K=2.5 \implies 10-x=5+2.5x\implies 5=3.5x\implies x=\frac{10}{7}\implies[\text{n-butane}]=3.43M,\text{ } [\text{isobutane}]=8.57M{}$

## Reaction 2: nitrogen + oxygen ⇌ nitric oxide

**5.** Consider the following reaction and its equilibrium constant:

$$\mathrm{N_2(g) + O_2(g)} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{2NO(g)} \qquad K_c = 4.0\times10^{-4} \text{ at } 2000\ \mathrm{K}$$

```smiles
N#N.O=O>>[N]=O.[N]=O
```
*N₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2 NO*

**a.** The reaction mixture contains 0.5 M NO, 0.5 M O₂ and 0.5 M N₂. What is $Q_c$ for this reaction?

**Answer:** ${}Q_{C}=\frac{[\text{NO}]^{2}}{[\text{N}_{2}][\text{O}_{2}]}=\frac{0.5^{2}}{(0.5)(0.5)}=\frac{0.5^{2}}{0.5^{2}}=0.5^{0}=1{}$

**b.** Is the reaction at equilibrium?

**Answer:** no, ${}Q\neq K, \text{ } Q>K{}$

**c.** If the reaction is not at equilibrium, which way will it go to establish equilibrium?

**Answer:** reaction moves toward reactant side

**d.** What are the equilibrium concentrations of all three components after equilibrium is established?

**Answer:** ${}\begin{bmatrix}R & N_{2} & O_{2} & NO \\  I & 0.5 & 0.5 & 0.5 \\  C & +x & +x & -2x \\  E & 0.5+x & 0.5+x & 0.5-2x\end{bmatrix}\implies \frac{(0.5-2x)^2}{(0.5+x)^2}=K=4.0\times 10^{-4}\implies \sqrt{ 4\times 10^{-4} }=\frac{0.5-2x}{0.5+x}\implies x=0.243\implies [N_{2}]=[O_{2}]=0.743M, \text{ } [NO]=0.0149M{}$
