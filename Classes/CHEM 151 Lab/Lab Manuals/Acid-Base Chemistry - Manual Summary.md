# Acid-Base Chemistry (Week of 10/26)
> **What you do:** Titrate a weak acid with a strong base while a pH probe records the curve, then measure the pH of NaCl, NH₄Cl and NaF solutions.
> **Goal:** Find the acid's K_a (pH = pKa at the half-equivalence point) and see why some salts make solutions acidic or basic.
> **Key idea:** Henderson–Hasselbalch: pH = pKa + log([A⁻]/[HA])
