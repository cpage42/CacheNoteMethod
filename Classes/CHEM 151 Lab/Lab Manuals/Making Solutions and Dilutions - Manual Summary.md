# Making Solutions and Dilutions (Done 9/28)
> **What you do:** Weigh solids into volumetric flasks to make stock solutions of Fe³⁺ and SCN⁻, then dilute them for the next lab.
> **Goal:** Practice getting concentrations right and understand stock versus working solutions.
> **Key idea:** M₁V₁ = M₂V₂; mass = M × V × molar mass
