# Intermolecular Forces
> **What you do:** Wet a temperature probe with alcohols and alkanes and record how far the temperature drops as each liquid evaporates.
> **Goal:** Rank the strength of hydrogen bonding and dispersion forces: stronger forces mean slower evaporation and a smaller drop.
> **Key idea:** Larger molar mass → stronger dispersion forces; H–O bonds → hydrogen bonding
