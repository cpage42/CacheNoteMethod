# Thermochemistry (Calorimetry)
> **What you do:** Drop heated metals into water in a calorimeter, then burn a Frito and a Cheeto under a can of water and measure the temperature rise.
> **Goal:** Identify metals from their specific heat and find the energy content of food, then compare with the bag's value (percent error).
> **Key idea:** q = C_s · m · ΔT; q_metal = −q_water
