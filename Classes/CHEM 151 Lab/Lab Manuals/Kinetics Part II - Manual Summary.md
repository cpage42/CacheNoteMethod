# Kinetics Part II (Week of 11/16)
> **What you do:** Repeat one mixture at a cold, room-temperature and warm bath and find k at each temperature.
> **Goal:** Find the activation energy E_a from the slope of ln k versus 1/T.
> **Key idea:** ln k = −(E_a/R)(1/T) + ln A
