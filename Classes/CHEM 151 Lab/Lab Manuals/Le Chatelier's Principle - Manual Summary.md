# Le Chatelier's Principle
> **What you do:** Disturb four equilibria (chromate/dichromate, acetic acid with methyl orange, CoCl₄²⁻, NH₄Cl) with acid, base, concentration or heat and watch the color or solid change.
> **Goal:** See that a system at equilibrium shifts to oppose any change, and decide which direction each reaction shifted.
> **Key idea:** Le Chatelier's principle
