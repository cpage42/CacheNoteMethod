# Electrochemistry (Week of 11/2)
> **What you do:** React metals with HCl, an aluminum can with CuCl₂ and sodium with water, then build a Zn/Cu cell and measure its voltage as you precipitate Cu²⁺ or Zn²⁺.
> **Goal:** Connect metal reactivity to standard reduction potentials and see that cell voltage measures how spontaneous a reaction is.
> **Key idea:** E°cell = E°red(cathode) − E°red(anode); Nernst: E = E° − (RT/nF) ln Q
