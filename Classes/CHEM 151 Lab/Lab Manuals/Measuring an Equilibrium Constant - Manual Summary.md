# Measuring an Equilibrium Constant (Done 10/5)
> **What you do:** Mix Fe³⁺ and SCN⁻ to make red FeSCN²⁺, build a Beer's law calibration, then measure absorbance of three test mixtures.
> **Goal:** Use an ICE table to calculate the equilibrium constant K_c for Fe³⁺ + SCN⁻ ⇌ FeSCN²⁺.
> **Key idea:** K_c = [FeSCN²⁺] / ([Fe³⁺][SCN⁻])
