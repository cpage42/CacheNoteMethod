## Pre lab calculations: solns, weight needed
**1. 100 mL 0.1 M Fe³⁺ soln in nitric acid from Fe(NO₃)₃·9H₂O**
- Fe → 1 · 55.845 g/mol
- N → 3 · 14.007 g/mol = 42.021 g/mol
- O → 9 · 15.999 g/mol = 143.991 g/mol
- H₂O → 9 · 18.015 g/mol = 162.135 g/mol
- Fe(NO₃)₃·9H₂O = 403.99 g/mol
- n = MV = 0.1 mol/L · 0.1 L = 0.01 mol Fe³⁺
- mass = n · mm = 0.01 · 403.99 = **4.04 g**

**2. 100 mL 20 mM Fe³⁺ soln in nitric acid from same**
mass = 4.04 · (20·10⁻³ …) = **0.808 g** [scaled from the 0.1 M solution]

**3. 100 mL 20 mM SCN⁻ soln from KSCN in nit. acid**
- mm = 39.1 + 32.06 + 12.01 + 14.01 = 97.18 g/mol
- n = MV = 0.02 mol/L · 0.1 L = 0.002 mol SCN⁻
- mass = n · mm = 0.002 mol · 97.18 g/mol = **0.194 g**

## Dilutions
M₁V₁ = M₂V₂ → V₁ = (M₂V₂)/M₁
- a. V₁ = (8 mM · 50 mL)/20 mM = **20 mL** → 50 mL of 8 mM Fe³⁺
- b. V₁ = (6 mM · 50 mL)/20 mM = **15 mL** → 50 mL of 6 mM Fe³⁺
- c. V₁ = (4 mM · 50 mL)/20 mM = **10 mL** → 50 mL of 4 mM Fe³⁺
- d. V₁ = (0.2 mM · 100 mL)/20 mM = **1 mL** → 100 mL of 0.2 mM SCN⁻
