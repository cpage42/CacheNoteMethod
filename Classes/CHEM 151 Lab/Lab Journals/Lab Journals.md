## Transcription notes
- Text is copied as written, including the original's errors. Unclear handwriting is marked [?].
- The IMF upload also has 2 pages of Logger Pro graphs (Figures 1–6 and a ΔT vs molar mass plot); these are not reproduced. The Colligative upload has a third page that did not render, likely the cooling-curve graphs.
- Items worth a second look when studying: Le Chatelier pre-lab writes sulfate as SO₄⁻ (it is SO₄²⁻). The Le Chatelier methyl orange paragraph says the forward reaction was favored on adding acetate, but adding acetate drives the equilibrium back toward acetic acid and lowers [H⁺]. The Colligative i values are negative because ΔT was taken as T_solution − T_water; the manual defines ΔT_fp = T_solvent − T_solution, which gives i ≈ 1.19 (sucrose) and 1.93 (NaCl). The Equilibrium Constant K_c error was caught and corrected in the notebook itself.
