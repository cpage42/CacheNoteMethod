**Table 5: Water data collection**

### Calculations
q_H₂O = m·q_food  [as written; q_H₂O = −q_food]
q_solution = C_s · m · ΔT, where C_s = 4.184

- q_fritos = 4.184 · 50 g · 7 °C = 1464.4 J
- q_cheetos = 4.184 · 50 g · 8 °C = 1673.6 J
- q_fritos = (1464.4 J)(1 cal / 4.184 J) = 350 cal = **0.350 Cal**
- q_cheetos = (1673.6 J)(1 cal / 4.184 J) = 400 cal = **0.400 Cal**
- (Cal/g)_fritos = 0.350 / 0.35 g = **1 Cal/g**
- (Cal/g)_cheetos = 0.400 / 0.46 g = **0.87 Cal/g**
- (% err)_fritos = (1 − 5.71)/5.71 · 100% = **82.49 % error**
- (% err)_cheetos = (0.87 − 5.71)/5.71 · 100% = **84.76 % error**

**Table 3: water data collection**

| Unknown metal # | V_w (mL) | m_w (g) | T_init | T_final | ΔT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 100 mL | 100 g | 24 °C | 25 °C | 1 °C |
| 5 | 100 mL | 100 g | 23.5 °C (24 °C crossed out) | 25 °C | 1.5 °C |

### Calculations
q_metal = −q_water
(m_metal)(C_s,metal)(ΔT_metal) = −(m_water)(C_s,water)(ΔT_water)
C_s,metal = −[(m_water)(C_s,water)(ΔT_water)] / [(m_metal)(ΔT_metal)]

- C_s,3 = −(100 g)(4.184 J/g°C)(1 °C) / [(49.94 g)(−69.5 °C)] = **0.121 J/g°C**
- C_s,5 = −(100 g)(4.184 J/g°C)(1.5 °C) / [(50.03 g)(−69.5 °C)] · −1 = **0.18 J/g°C**  [sign handling as written]
