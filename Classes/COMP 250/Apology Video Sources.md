# Apology Video Sources (Essay 2)
Class: [[COMP 250]] · Matrix: [[Genre Analysis Matrix]] · Genre: **the YouTube creator apology video**

All eight are original uploads, still on the creators' own channels as of Oct 13, 2026. Descriptions are quoted exactly as posted. Views were counted on that date.

> [!tip] Classification chain (Matrix Q1)
> public apology → online video → YouTube creator-to-audience content → **YouTube creator apology video** → [subgenre]
> Subgenre axes: **trigger** (resurfaced past / recent incident / allegations / broken promise to fans), **mode** (confessional / scripted accountability / corporate / parody), **commercial stance** (plugs / stripped / demonetized).

---

## S1. Jeffree Star, "RACISM." (Jun 20, 2017)
[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Su6FeI7lHVg) · 15 min · 5.3M views
- **What for:** old videos and recordings resurfaced in which he used racial slurs and made racist remarks.
- **Subgenre:** resurfaced past · confessional · monetized
- **Description:**
> I owe it to all of you to be brutally honest about my past, and I'm here today to discuss old videos of me that have been resurfacing lately. I want to clear the air about drama, rumors and being accused of being "racist"... I love and respect all of you for allowing me to be honest and real with you guys. I may not always be understood, but you get ME. […] With having such a huge social media platform, I've decided to start being 100% OPEN and talk about some serious subjects and my mistakes. Thank you for listening.
> *SHOP: http://www.jeffreestarcosmetics.com […]
- **Notes:** "racist" is in quotation marks, framing it as an accusation. Apology and shop links sit in the same block of text.

## S2. Tana Mongeau, "An apology" (Feb 17, 2017)
[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fazh9Lm1kDE) · 23 min · 4.2M views
- **What for:** using a racial slur. In a January 2017 video she discussed the word, and another YouTuber then publicized her own past use of it.
- **Subgenre:** recent incident · confessional/emotional · channel playlists and social links, no products
- **Description:**
> Thank you for hearing me out, thank you for supporting me even when I don't deserve it. I promise I will make it up to you […] I'm still young and an idiot and I'll probably make a million more mistakes- but I will own up to them in a courageous and honest fashion from this point forward. […] You guys are my family […] You guys truly saved my life, doing this saved my life. And I don't want to live a life where I can't do this. I don't want to live a life without you […] THANK YOU for holding me accountable […]
- **Notes:** the most melodramatic *sincere* description in the set. It's a letter to fans, with family language, promises about the future, and thanks to the audience for holding her accountable.

## S3. Tana Mongeau, "a long overdue apology" (Sep 4, 2020)
[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vwLiy4ti9is) · 15 min · 2.2M views
- **What for:** renewed accusations of racism in summer 2020, including from a Black former friend. It also apologized for her *earlier* apologies.
- **Subgenre:** resurfaced past + allegations · accountability · **explicitly demonetized**
- **Description:**
> join me in donating to the NAACP, alongside these other charities […] this video is 100% demonetized. petitions i signed and you can too (i will be updating this as i sign more […])
> *(followed by a long list of NAACP, BLM and change.org petition links)*
- **Notes:** almost no apology text. Sincerity is signaled through action (donations, petitions) and by giving up ad revenue. Compare with S2 from the same creator three years earlier: the genre's expectations had shifted.

## S4. James Charles, "An Open Conversation" (Jul 2, 2021)
[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fQvCZ5ZsnGM) · 30 min · 6.7M views
- **What for:** allegations that he sent sexual messages to young men, some of them minors. He admitted messaging minors and said he hadn't known their ages.
- **Subgenre:** allegations · scripted/therapeutic accountability · no plugs
- **Description:**
> Hi Sisters, long time no see. After a lot of self reflection and many conversations with friends, family, role models in my industry, and professionals, I've come to terms with several issues I've been ignoring for a long time now. In today's video, I wanted to have an honest conversation and hope to answer some questions, share my progress and talk about what I've been up to over the past couple of months with anyone who wants to listen.
- **Notes:** opens with his channel's signature greeting. The vocabulary is therapeutic ("self reflection," "come to terms," "share my progress"), and it's framed as a *conversation*, not a confession.

## S5. Logan Paul, "So Sorry." (Jan 2, 2018)
[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QwZT7T-TXT0) · 1 min 44 s · 65.0M views
- **What for:** filming and posting footage of a suicide victim's body in Japan's Aokigahara forest. This followed a written apology on Twitter that was widely criticized.
- **Subgenre:** recent incident · confessional, single take · **stripped (no plugs)**
- **Description:** *blank*
- **Notes:** the reference point for the whole genre, and by far the most viewed in this set. The description is completely empty: no merch, no links, no text. His regular vlogs at the time were full of merch links, so the blank box is a deliberate choice. Compare with S1 (Jeffree Star, plugs kept) and S3 (Tana, "100% demonetized" stated outright). Also compare the format: a 1:44 single take versus 15–30 minute sit-downs.

## S6. Jaclyn Hill, "My Lipsticks" (Jun 12, 2019)
[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x8QeSZpr0bs) · 14 min · 7.7M views
- **What for:** customers reported hairs, holes, fuzz and grit in her debut lipstick line, and some suspected mold. She denied mold, blamed manufacturing and offered refunds.
- **Subgenre:** recent incident · **corporate/brand apology** · customer service
- **Description:**
> Thank you for your feedback & constructive criticism. If any one of you are not satisfied with your purchase, please contact help@jaclyncosmetics.com
> This video has been revised at the 2:41 mark in adherence with our partner's confidentiality agreement. SGS does not permit the disclosure of documents with redactions
- **Notes:** a hybrid of creator apology and corporate statement. The language is customer service plus legal; there are no feelings in the text.

## S7. Nikita Dragun, "An Apology. I AM SO SORRY!!" (Aug 16, 2017)
[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nI9cOYXxE88) · 16 min · 1.8M views
- **What for:** nothing. It's clickbait wrapped around a reality-show-parody vlog.
- **Subgenre:** **parody/clickbait** · monetized
- **Description:**
> I AM SO SORRY! I APOLOGIZE....................................................................................................................................................for using CLICKBAIT. This is real, this is me. […] Here is an unfiltered, unedited, and unintended Dragun Tales reality show PARODY. […]
> DISCOUNT CODES: Morphe Brushes […] Lilly Lashes […]
- **Notes:** evidence that "An Apology." in a title was a known formula by 2017, one you could use as bait.

## S8. Manny MUA, "We NEED to talk... Im sorry" (Jun 27, 2018)
[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AuT6WuCYttk) · 12 min · 2.8M views
- **What for:** going quiet. It's an emotional "opening up" video about his struggles, with the apology aimed at the relationship with his audience rather than at a wrongdoing.
- **Subgenre:** relationship/absence · confessional, **melodramatic** · monetized
- **Description:**
> Hey guys just waned to post this video cause it was time for me to open up as Manny not just Manny Mua, life is HARD and I want you guys to know that you are not alone. […] I love you all so much and I hope this video about me opening up can help you in any way […] Its a constant battle every day, its hard... but we will get through it! I love you.
> I CREATED A BRAND! CHECK OUT LIFES A DRAG HERE - Lunarbeauty.com […]
> I love you guys.
- **Notes:** caps for emotional emphasis ("HARD," "REAL") and direct address ("you are not alone"), then a hard cut to a product plug, with "I love you" on both sides of it.

| [Nikocado Avocado, "i'm sorry" (2020)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gFEjCuae8RA) | Unclear (a mukbang) | "I'm so sorry. I am very sorry. I hope you can forgive me. My apology...." then a giveaway and merch |
| Laura Lee, "My Apology" (2018), [reupload with original captions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NYVmWxitVSQ) | Racist tweets from 2012; lost sponsors | Original deleted. Survives only as reuploads by others, and viewers trolled its captions |

**Pattern worth arguing:** the highest-profile apologies strip the description (blank, or a single period) with no plugs, while ordinary uploads on the same channels are full of links. Having nothing there is itself a convention: it signals "this isn't content." Tana's "100% demonetized" says it outright. Videos that keep the plugs (Jeffree Star, Manny MUA) blur the line between apologizing and selling.
