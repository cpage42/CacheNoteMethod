**1. (2.1–2.2) Linear, exponential or neither?**

Decide for each table. Justify using differences or ratios.

| $x$ | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) $f(x)$ | 2 | 5 | 8 | 11 | 14 |
| (b) $g(x)$ | 3 | 6 | 12 | 24 | 48 |
| (c) $h(x)$ | 1 | 4 | 9 | 16 | 25 |

(d) Write a formula for the one that is exponential.

**2. (2.3) Exponential function features**

$f(x) = 200(0.85)^x$

(a) Is this growth or decay? By what percent does $f$ change each time $x$ increases by 1?

(b) Find $f(3)$. *(Calculator OK)*

(c) Describe the end behavior: $\lim_{x\to\infty} f(x)$ and $\lim_{x\to-\infty} f(x)$.

(d) What is the horizontal asymptote?

**3. (2.4) Exponential model in context**

A city has 50,000 people and its population **decreases by 3% each year**.

(a) Write a model $P(t)$ for the population after $t$ years.

(b) Find the population after 10 years. *(Calculator OK)*

(c) Use logarithms to find when the population will be half of its original size. *(Calculator OK)*

**4. (2.5) Choosing a model**

Two models were fit to the same 7 data points. Their residuals are below.

| $x$ | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Model A (linear) residual | 5 | 1 | −2 | −3 | −2 | 1 | 5 |
| Model B (exponential) residual | 1 | −2 | 1 | 2 | −1 | −2 | 1 |

(a) Which model is a better fit? Explain using the *pattern* of the residuals.

(b) What does a residual of $-3$ mean?

**5. (2.6) Composition**

Let $f(x) = 2x + 1$ and $g(x) = x^2 - 3$.

(a) Find $f(g(2))$.

(b) Find $g(f(x))$ and simplify.

(c) Is $f(g(x)) = g(f(x))$? Explain.

**6. (2.7) Inverse functions**

(a) Find $f^{-1}(x)$ for $f(x) = 3x - 6$.

(b) A one-to-one function has $f(2) = 7$ and $f(5) = -1$. Find $f^{-1}(7)$ and $f^{-1}(-1)$.

(c) Does $h(x) = x^2$ (all real numbers) have an inverse function? Explain.

**7. (2.8) Evaluate without a calculator**

(a) $\log_2 32$  (b) $\log_5\dfrac{1}{25}$  (c) $\log_{1/2} 8$  (d) $\ln e^4$  (e) $\log 0.001$

**8. (2.10) A logarithmic function**

$g(x) = \log_3(x - 2) + 1$

(a) State the domain and the vertical asymptote.

(b) Find $g(5)$.

(c) Find the $x$-intercept.

**9. (2.11) Properties of logarithms**

(a) Expand: $\log_2\!\left(\dfrac{8x^3}{y}\right)$

(b) Write as a single logarithm: $3\log x - 2\log y + \log 4$

(c) True or false: $\log(a + b) = \log a + \log b$. Explain.

**10. (2.12) Solving equations**

Solve each. Give exact answers; also a decimal for (b) and (d). *(Calculator OK for decimals only)*

(a) $3^{2x - 1} = 81$

(b) $7^x = 20$

(c) $\log_2 x + \log_2(x - 2) = 3$

(d) $\ln(x + 1) = 2$

**11. (2.13–2.14) Half-life and semi-log**

A sample has 80 grams of a substance with a **half-life of 6 days**.

(a) Write a model $A(t)$ for the mass after $t$ days.

(b) How much remains after 15 days? *(Calculator OK)*

(c) When will 5 grams remain?

(d) A semi-log plot shows $\log_2 A$ versus $t$. What kind of graph is it, and what is its slope?

---

## Answer key

**1.** (a) linear (differences all 3); (b) exponential (ratios all 2); (c) neither (differences 3, 5, 7, 9 are not constant and ratios are not constant). (d) $g(x) = 3\cdot 2^x$.

**2.** (a) decay, 15% decrease; (b) $\approx 122.8$; (c) $\to 0$ as $x\to\infty$, $\to\infty$ as $x\to-\infty$; (d) $y = 0$.

**3.** (a) $P(t) = 50000(0.97)^t$; (b) $\approx 36{,}871$; (c) $t = \dfrac{\ln 0.5}{\ln 0.97} \approx 22.8$ years.

**4.** (a) Model B: its residuals are scattered with no pattern. Model A's residuals form a U-shape, so a line is the wrong kind of function. (b) Residual = actual − predicted, so the model overestimated that point by 3.

**5.** (a) 3; (b) $g(f(x)) = 4x^2 + 4x - 2$; (c) No: $f(g(x)) = 2x^2 - 5$ is different.

**6.** (a) $f^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{x+6}{3}$; (b) $2$ and $5$; (c) No: it fails the horizontal line test ($h(2) = h(-2)$).

**7.** (a) 5; (b) $-2$; (c) $-3$; (d) 4; (e) $-3$.

**8.** (a) domain $x > 2$; vertical asymptote $x = 2$; (b) $g(5) = 2$; (c) $x = \dfrac{7}{3}$.

**9.** (a) $3 + 3\log_2 x - \log_2 y$; (b) $\log\dfrac{4x^3}{y^2}$; (c) False: the product rule says $\log(ab) = \log a + \log b$; counterexample $a = b = 1$: $\log 2 \neq 0$.

**10.** (a) $x = \frac52$; (b) $x = \dfrac{\ln 20}{\ln 7} \approx 1.54$; (c) $x = 4$ (reject $-2$); (d) $x = e^2 - 1 \approx 6.39$.

**11.** (a) $A(t) = 80\left(\tfrac12\right)^{t/6}$; (b) $\approx 14.1$ g; (c) $t = 24$ days; (d) a straight line with slope $-\tfrac16$.
