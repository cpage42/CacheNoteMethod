# Review Problems: Polynomial and Rational Functions
Tutoring: [[Client 2]] · Printable: `PDFs/Client 2 - Intuition Worksheet Unit 2 Summative.pdf` (solutions on the last page)

Built from [[Client 2/Unit 2 Summative/1 - In-Person Worksheet|1 - In-Person Worksheet]], redesigned around intuition (graph matching, evaluating terms and functions to see end behavior, asymptotes and holes emerge) and formatted as a standard college-style problem set. About an hour with help.

## Part I: Polynomial Functions
1. Match four polynomial graphs to factored forms; state the deciding feature.
2. $f(x)=2x^7-5x^4+x$: evaluate each term at $x=\pm10$; end behavior.
3. $g(x)=(1-2x)^3(x+5)^2$: degree, leading coefficient, end behavior.
4. Factor $2x^3+3x^2-8x-12$; intercepts; verify one.
5. Synthetic division: $p(x)=3x^3+7x^2-7x-3$, zero at $x=-3$.
6. $y=x^2+6x+25$: vertex form, then solve; connect.
7. Zeros $-2$ and $1+3i$: conjugate, polynomial, intercepts.
8. Sign table for $(x-1)^n$ near $x=1$; multiplicity rule.
9. Build a polynomial: crosses at $-3$, touches at $1$, $y$-intercept $(0,6)$; sketch.

## Part II: Rational Functions
10. $r(x)=\frac{2x+4}{x-1}$: value table far out and near $x=1$; asymptotes.
11. $r(x)=\frac{3x^2-3x-6}{x^2-4}$: factor, evaluate near $\pm2$, hole vs vertical asymptote, characteristics table.
12. Three limits at infinity.
13. Match three rational graphs.
14. $r(x)=\frac{x-2}{x^2-1}$: asymptotes, intercepts, sign chart, one-sided limits, sketch.

## Solutions
1. (a) B (b) D (c) A (d) C.
2. $f(10)=19{,}950{,}010$, $f(-10)=-20{,}050{,}010$; $\infty$ and $-\infty$.
3. $-8x^5$; $\lim_{x\to\infty}=-\infty$, $\lim_{x\to-\infty}=\infty$.
4. $(2x+3)(x-2)(x+2)$.
5. $(x+3)(3x+1)(x-1)$.
6. $(x+3)^2+16$, no intercepts; $x=-3\pm4i$.
7. $1-3i$; $x^3+6x+20$; one intercept.
8. Odd multiplicity crosses, even touches.
9. $2(x+3)(x-1)^2$.
10. 2.667, 2.061, 2.006; 62, 602, 6002; −58, −598. HA $y=2$, VA $x=1$.
11. Hole $(2,\tfrac94)$, VA $x=-2$, HA $y=3$, domain $x\ne\pm2$, $x$-int $(-1,0)$, $y$-int $(0,\tfrac32)$.
12. $\tfrac52$, $0$, $-\infty$.
13. (a) B (b) C (c) A.
14. VA $x=\pm1$, HA $y=0$, $(2,0)$, $(0,2)$; signs $-,+,-,+$; crosses HA at $x=2$.
